What it does

A blank slate that learns you, not a template.

No borrowed voice, no hook formulas, no assumptions about your niche. Everything comes from your answers and your own writing.

Interviews you first

Your business, what you're working on now and next, who you want to reach, and the clients you want to attract.

Writes in your voice

It studies posts, emails, and notes you've written, then tests a draft until you say “that sounds like me.”

Value-first posts

Real checklists, how-tos, and lessons, each ending with an easy call to action. No “comment for the link.”

Builds your calendar

Content pillars, a weekly rhythm you choose, and a rolling 2–4 week calendar tied to your real launches and events.

Profile and company page

Post as yourself, as your business page, or both, with different content for each if you want.

Safe by design

Never asks for your password, never posts without your OK, and stays inside the platform's rules. No bots, pods, or auto-DMs.

Built-in restraint

Give before you ask. Every time.

Most of your posts just help people. If you have something to offer, like a service, a free resource, or a booking link, you decide how often it comes up. Or never.

Questions

Good to know

What do I need to use it?

A Claude account that supports Skills. It works in Claude Code (terminal, desktop app, or IDE) and in Claude.ai. Automatic posting through the included scripts needs Claude Code or a computer that can run Python, but the other posting methods work anywhere.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Claude walks you through everything in plain English. If “just tell me what to click” is your style, it'll recommend the simplest setup, where Claude writes and you click schedule.

Will the posts sound like AI?

The skill starts with no voice of its own. It learns from samples of your writing, tests a draft with you, and keeps a voice guide so every post sounds like you.

Does it post without asking me?

Never. You see the final text, image, destination, and time before anything is published or scheduled.

Is my account safe?

Claude never asks for your password. Logins go through the platform's official sign-in screen, Zapier, or your scheduling tool. It won't use bots, engagement pods, scraping, or automated DMs.

Can it post to my company page?

Yes, if you're an admin of the page. The setup guide explains which posting methods support Pages.

Is it really free?

Yes. Enter your email, download the zip, and it's yours.
