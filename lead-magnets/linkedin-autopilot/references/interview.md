# Interview question bank

Ask one round at a time. Keep it to 3–5 questions per message, stay friendly, and
adapt the wording to what they've already told you. If an answer covers a later
question, skip that one. After each round, give a short "Here's what I heard"
summary, ask them to correct anything, then save it to the right file.

Open with something like:

> "Before I write anything, I want to understand your business and what you want
> LinkedIn to do for you. I'll ask a few short rounds of questions. Skip anything
> you don't know yet. Rough answers, links, and pasted notes all work."

---

## Round 1: The business → `profile.md`

1. What does your business do, in a sentence or two? (Paste your website or LinkedIn "About" if that's easier.)
2. Who are you? Your name, role, and why someone should listen to you on this topic (experience, results, story).
3. What are you working on **right now**? (Launches, projects, client work, things you're building.)
4. What's coming up in the next 3–6 months? (New offers, events, a book, hiring, a rebrand, a slow season.)
5. What do you want LinkedIn to do for you? Rank them: leads/clients, email list growth, authority/visibility, hiring, partnerships, speaking, sales of a specific product.

## Round 2: Who you want to attract → `profile.md`

1. Describe your ideal client: job title or type of person, industry, company size, location.
2. What problem are they trying to solve when they find you? What do they say, in their own words?
3. What makes them ready to buy? (A trigger event, a deadline, a pain getting worse.)
4. Who **isn't** a fit? Anyone you'd rather not attract?
5. Is anyone besides buyers worth reaching? (Referral partners, investors, recruits, media.)

## Round 3: Offers → `profile.md`

1. List your products and services. For each one: what it is, who it's for, the price or price range, and how someone buys (book a call, checkout link, DM, application).
2. Which ones do you most want to sell in the next 90 days?
3. Do you have results you can share? Testimonials, case studies, numbers, before/after. (Only ones you have permission to use.)
4. Anything you can't say publicly (pricing, client names, compliance limits)?

## Round 4: Lead magnets and funnel → `lead-magnets.md`

1. Do you have any free resources people can download or join? (Guides, checklists, templates, a mini-course, a quiz, a webinar, a free consult, a community.) For each one: name, link, and who it's for.
2. Where do sign-ups go? (Kit/ConvertKit, Mailchimp, Beehiiv, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Flodesk, Systeme, Kajabi, something else, or nothing yet.)
3. Is there an email sequence after someone opts in? What does it lead to? (A call, a purchase, a waitlist.)
4. How do you want people to get the freebie from a post?
   - Link in the post
   - Link in the first comment
   - "Comment [WORD] and I'll send it" (you send the DM yourself; see `funnels.md`)
   - Link in your profile's Featured section or custom button
   - Not sure, recommend one
5. Want help coming up with a new lead magnet? (If yes, brainstorm 3–5 ideas tied to Round 2 pain points after the interview.)

## Round 5: Content preferences → `pillars.md`

1. What topics do you know cold, and could talk about forever?
2. Which kinds of posts appeal to you? Pick any: teaching or how-to, personal stories, behind-the-scenes, opinions or hot takes, client wins, industry news commentary, tips lists, carousels/document posts, short videos, polls, announcements, offer or launch posts, event promotion.
3. Is there anything you **won't** post about? (Politics, family, competitors, specific clients, money.)
4. How often do you realistically want to post, on which days, and at what times? Which time zone?
5. Are there LinkedIn creators whose content you like, or dislike? What specifically about them? (This is for inspiration on format only. Never copy their voice.)

## Round 6: Voice → `voice.md`

1. Paste 3–10 things **you** wrote that feel like you: past LinkedIn posts, emails, newsletter issues, website copy, even text messages. More is better.
2. Pick three words for how you want to come across. (For example: warm, blunt, nerdy, calm, funny, polished, scrappy.)
3. Emojis: none, a few, or plenty? Hashtags: none, 1–3, or more?
4. Words, phrases, or styles you love? Ones you hate or never want to see?
5. First person singular ("I") or company voice ("we")? Does that change between your personal profile and your Page?

> If they have no writing samples, offer to interview them out loud: ask them to
> explain one topic to you as if you were a client, then use their phrasing as
> the voice sample.

## Round 7: Media → `media-library.md` + `media/`

1. Do you have photos you'd like to use regularly? (Headshots, working shots, event photos, team, product, workspace.) Drop them into `linkedin-autopilot/media/`, or share a folder link.
2. Any brand assets? (Logo, colors, fonts, Canva templates, a brand kit.)
3. Any marketing materials worth reusing? (Slide decks, PDFs, videos, podcast episodes, blog posts, webinars.) These become a content mine.
4. Any visual inspiration? Accounts, posts, or styles you want images to feel like.
5. Should images include text overlays, or be clean photos? Do you use Canva or another design tool I could connect to?

After this round, list each file in `media-library.md` with a short description,
the pillar it fits, and any usage notes ("only use on Page", "client approved").

## Round 8: Destinations → `profile.md`

1. Should posts go to your **personal profile**, a **company Page**, or both?
2. If there's a Page: what's its name and URL, and are you a **Super admin** or **Content admin** on it? (You need one of those roles to post as the Page.)
3. If both: how should they differ? (A common setup is personal posts for stories and opinions, and Page posts for announcements, offers, and resharing.)
4. Do you want posts on the Page reshared from your profile, or the other way around?

## Round 9: Posting method → `profile.md`

1. How comfortable are you with tech? (1 = "just tell me what to click", 5 = "I'm fine with code and APIs")
2. Do you already use Zapier, Make, Buffer, Hootsuite, GoHighLevel, or another scheduler?
3. Do you want me to post **for** you automatically, or would you rather I write and you click "schedule"?
4. Budget for tools: $0, a small monthly fee, or whatever works best?

Then recommend a method from `posting-methods.md` and walk them through setup.

---

## When the interview is done

Give them a one-screen summary:
- Who they help and what they sell
- The goal LinkedIn serves
- 3–5 proposed pillars
- Their lead magnet(s) and CTA style
- Destinations and posting cadence
- Recommended posting method

Ask: "Anything wrong or missing?" Then move on to Phase 2.
